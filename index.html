About CTU Naga

Cebu Technological University - Naga Extension Campus is located in Central Poblacion, City of Naga, Cebu, Philippines. It is part of the Cebu Technological University system.

Our University

CTU brings together instruction, research, extension, and production. These areas help students develop knowledge and practical skills while contributing to their communities.
